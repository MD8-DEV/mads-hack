Revision Rounds - scope tracker for freelance web designers

Track revision rounds against contract limits in real time

For solo web designers on fixed-price projects who lose hours to client changes beyond the agreed scope.

What it is meant to do

  • Send clients a clear summary that shows scope used versus remaining
  • Stop working for free when clients exceed the agreed rounds
